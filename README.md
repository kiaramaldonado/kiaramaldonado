👋 Hi, I'm Kiara Maldonado, **SWE @ Dekbera Communications**.

I love tackling complex, creative projects and building software across web, desktop, and multiplatform technologies. It's my job, my hobby, my dream, and occasionally, my nightmare.

I joined this coding race around 2023, right as AI started galloping alongside it. I believe technology and science should be accessible to everyone — so people can learn, experiment, improve their work, and make their lives a little better. That's why I keep putting my creativity into building tools people can use for free, or for *la voluntad* ;) such as [**OS For Cosmos**](https://osforcosmos.org) 🌌

It's an open-source project built to help people get closer to the universe — by letting them explore, learn, and contribute. I feel like academics, engineers, and other experts often gatekeep fascinating things that more people should have the chance to explore. Knowledge shouldn't be locked away when we can make it accessible to everyone. Let's not gatekeep the universe.
